Lu Zou 邹露

Lecturer and Master's Supervisor
Wuhan Institute of Technology

I am a lecturer in the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Wuhan Institute of Technology. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from the University of Science and Technology of China in December 2023, advised by Prof. Zhangjin Huang and Prof. Naijie Gu. My research focuses on 3D vision and robot perception, with particular interests in category-level object pose estimation, point cloud understanding, human-centered vision, and multimodal intelligence.

Focus areas

Research

My group studies perception models that connect geometric structure, human motion, and semantic knowledge.

3D

3D Vision and Robotics

Object pose estimation, point cloud understanding, geometric representation learning, and robot perception.

H

Human-Centered Vision

Human pose estimation, multi-person motion forecasting, activity understanding, and interaction modeling.

M

Multimodal Intelligence

Vision-language representation, semantic guidance, multimodal fusion, and embodied perception.

I

Industrial Vision

Lightweight visual inspection, anomaly detection, weak supervision, and deployable vision systems.

Teaching and student supervision

I teach courses including Algorithm Design and Analysis, Data Structures Project, and Mobile Internet. I welcome students who are motivated to work on 3D computer vision, human-centered vision, and multimodal perception.
